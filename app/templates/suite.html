        INC-2026-11-04 · Database timeout

        Resolved · 18 min · 3.8% budget
        Summary
        On 4 Nov 2026 at 14:10 UTC an unindexed analytical query ran full table scans on /api/v1/items. The Cloud SQL PostgreSQL connection pool was exhausted within 45 seconds, cascading into HTTP 500s.
        Detection
        The multi-window FastErrorBudgetBurn alert fired at 45 seconds. The triage bot correlated structured logs with trace trc-03d12a and sent the mitigation to on-call.
        Corrective action
        Composite B-Tree index added, max_connections tuned and an adaptive circuit breaker enabled on the business endpoint.
